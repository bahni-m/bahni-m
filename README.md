### / my team

aibuilders.id runs on me plus 9 AI agents. Each one has one job: research and copy, content and visuals, sales, the numbers, ops. Every morning they report in and their work gets reviewed.

It's the setup I build for business owners at peakspec.xyz, with the same rule: I approve the steps that matter.
